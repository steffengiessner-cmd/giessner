About Steffen Giessner

Steffen R. Giessner is Professor of Organisational Behaviour and Change at Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University.

My path to research

I was born in East Germany. Living through German reunification shaped the questions I ask as a researcher today. When familiar institutions change, what gives people a sense of belonging? Who do they look to for leadership? Those questions run through my work on leadership, change, and identity.

I began my career in banking as a certified banking professional before I went on to study psychology. I earned an MSc in Psychology from the University of Kent at Canterbury and a PhD in Psychology from Friedrich Schiller University Jena. My work has appeared in journals in organisational behaviour, management, and psychology and has received international media attention.

A little about me

I’m German, a husband, and a father. I love spending time with my wife and son, listening to music, sailing, and going out with friends. At the university, one of my favourite parts of the day is having a good conversation with students and colleagues.
