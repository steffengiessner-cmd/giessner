Speaker

Leadership, Change, Diversity, and Identity

Steffen’s talks explore how people lead, work across differences, and find a sense of belonging as organisations change.

Speaking Themes

  • Leadership. How do voice and body language shape the way people perceive a leader, in person and online?
  • Change. How can leaders support employees when a merger reshapes their team or AI changes the way they work?
  • Diversity. Whose ideas get heard in a meeting, and how can teams draw on a wider range of perspectives?
  • Identity. When two teams come together, what helps people begin to think of themselves as “we”?
