Research

Research Agenda

Steffen studies leadership, change, and identity in organisations. His work explores how people respond when their work changes, who they look to for guidance, and what gives them a sense of belonging.

Leadership

Follower Perceptions and Leader Behaviour

How do employees recognise a leader? This work examines what shapes leader behaviour and how followers interpret cues such as body language and tone of voice.

Change

How Employees Experience Organisational Change

What helps employees make sense of change and decide whether to support it? Steffen explores this question across different kinds of organisational change. Mergers are one example. A current research interest is how employees respond when organisations introduce artificial intelligence (AI) into their work.

Identity

Belonging After a Merger

When two organisations merge, who do employees mean when they say “we”? This research explores how ties to a former organisation shape identification with the new one.
